Photo crowdsourcing

This page manages street-part photo submissions. A computer-vision model performs the first layer of approval, then a human-in-the-loop staff team from the relevant customer company reviews the photo before it becomes active evidence.

UploadCV first reviewStaff reviewApproved

1. Upload

Users submit a clearer street-part photo from the platform settings or this page.

2. CV first review

The model checks resolution, exposure, blur, street-view aspect, and evidence usefulness.

3. Staff review

Selected admins from customer companies receive the photo for human review.

4. Active evidence

Approved photos become the active street-view evidence for JalanLens users.
