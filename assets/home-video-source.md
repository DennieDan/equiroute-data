# Home background video source

The JalanLens homepage background is a 25s muted 1280x720 loop stitched from five 5-second Pexels clips, matching the login-page stock-video workflow but using real city/footpath/pedestrian footage instead of generated abstract shapes.

Sources used:

1. Pexels — "Urban City Traffic in Singapore Daytime" by Shlok Rana — https://www.pexels.com/video/urban-city-traffic-in-singapore-daytime-34631630/
2. Pexels — "Traffic At Night On A Street In Singapore" by Ivan Yeo — https://www.pexels.com/video/city-cars-walking-town-4455203/
3. Pexels — "Busy Urban Pathway with Diverse Pedestrians" by Sururi Ballıdağ Director — https://www.pexels.com/video/busy-urban-pathway-with-diverse-pedestrians-35214546/
4. Pexels — "Pedestrians Crossing Urban Street in Daylight" by SHOX ART — https://www.pexels.com/video/pedestrians-crossing-urban-street-in-daylight-36337623/
5. Pexels — "Busy Urban Street Scene with Crosswalk" by Marc Barcelona — https://www.pexels.com/video/busy-urban-street-scene-with-crosswalk-36487135/

Local derivatives:

- `jalanlens-home-city-loop.mp4` — 25s muted H.264 loop, 1280x720.
- `jalanlens-home-city-loop.webm` — 25s muted VP9 loop, 1280x720.
- `jalanlens-home-city-poster.jpg` — poster frame for the homepage video.
