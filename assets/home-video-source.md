# Home background video source

The JalanLens homepage background is a 25s muted 1280x720 loop stitched from five 5-second Pexels clips. All five clips are Singapore footage, matching the login-page stock-video workflow while replacing abstract/generated shapes with real city, street, pedestrian, and landmark scenes.

Sources used:

1. Pexels — "Urban City Traffic in Singapore Daytime" by Shlok Rana — https://www.pexels.com/video/urban-city-traffic-in-singapore-daytime-34631630/
2. Pexels — "Traffic At Night On A Street In Singapore" by Ivan Yeo — https://www.pexels.com/video/city-cars-walking-town-4455203/
3. Pexels — "People are standing in front of a food stand" by LayG Traveller — https://www.pexels.com/video/people-are-standing-in-front-of-a-food-stand-17257780/
4. Pexels — "Aerial View of Busy Singapore Overpass" by Florian Delée — https://www.pexels.com/video/aerial-view-of-busy-singapore-overpass-35200144/
5. Pexels — "Young Woman Walking at Singapore Merlion Park" by Tue Simon — https://www.pexels.com/video/young-woman-walking-at-singapore-merlion-park-32810484/

Local derivatives:

- `jalanlens-home-city-loop.mp4` — 25s muted H.264 loop, 1280x720.
- `jalanlens-home-city-loop.webm` — 25s muted VP9 loop, 1280x720.
